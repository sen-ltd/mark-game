Mark subtract one, or halve

A number n. On your turn, replace it by n − 1 or by ⌊n/2⌋. Whoever moves to 0 wins. Fraenkel (2011): you are lost exactly when n written in binary ends in an odd number of 0s (a dopey number; the others are vile). The Grundy value is just as simple — 1 on vile numbers with an odd number of 1s, 2 on those with an even number — so sums of games are solved too. Mark-t subtracts 1…t−1 or divides by t (same rule in base t), MiMark is the misère game, UpMark rounds the half up. Although every move only subtracts, the P-positions are not periodic.
